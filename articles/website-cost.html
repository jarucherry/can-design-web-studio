How much does a shop website cost, and what to ask before you hire

Website prices run from a few thousand baht to hundreds of thousands. What makes the difference is what the site can do and what you get to keep. These are the questions to ask anyone before you decide.

Website prices in Thailand run from under a thousand baht to hundreds of thousands, so the number alone tells you little. What matters is what the site does for your shop, and what you keep once it is handed over.

Rough prices by kind of site

A one-page site from a ready-made design suits a shop that wants its details, menu, map and contact on the web; it usually costs a few thousand baht. A site designed for your brand, with features such as a price calculator or booking, runs from several thousand to tens of thousands. An online shop with a cart, stock and an admin system usually starts in the tens of thousands.

Questions to ask before you hire

Can I see work that actually runs, not just screenshots?

Whose name are the site and domain registered in, and can I move them elsewhere one day?

What will I pay next year — domain, hosting, maintenance?

How many rounds of changes are included, and can I edit the site myself afterwards?

Does it work well on a phone? Most customers will open it there.

In short
